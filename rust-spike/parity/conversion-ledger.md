## Rust fixes

None. Every converted case matched on the first complete comparison once the case itself was right. All case failures during conversion were mistakes in the case (wrong guess at ordering, a probe the app role cannot run, ids or ties), not differences between the servers.

## Harness notes

No harness changes. Limits found:

- No step can queue a job directly or read either server's own job payload (tests 649 and 693).
- No way to run SQL as the superuser or add a policy mid-case, so the negative control in task-summary 109 cannot be written.
- The create route cannot set position or status suggested, so those cases seed rows by SQL.

## What it would take to bring each outside group in

- Recurrence roll-forward and schedules: port roll-forward, next-instance creation, the materialize job and the schedule reconcile.
- Breakdown: port the breakdown route and its model call.
- External-key suggestions and email feedback: port the source and external-key columns, the uniqueness rules, and the feedback port.
- Task summary: port the summary builder and its chat registration.
- Action policy and agency auto-execute: port the policy self-heal and the trust setting route.
