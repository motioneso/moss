`[location.pathname, location.search]` effect re-runs. Each effect may recreate its
listener-local factory, but it must receive the same ref-held clock. Cleanup may stop
the pending timer, but must not reset the last-upload clock. Preserve the pre-upload
timestamp assignment and remaining-interval calculation.

Add a unit test that schedules an upload, stops the subscription, creates the
resubscription behavior, and proves a second schedule respects the remaining
`minIntervalMs`; it must fail against the current per-effect factory reset.
