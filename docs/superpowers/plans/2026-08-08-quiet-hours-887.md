## Decision

In the existing `normal notification deferred during active quiet hours; hidden from listVisible` test, import Vitest's `vi`, fake only `Date`, and set a fixed instant at `2099-08-08T23:58:30.000Z`. Restore real timers in `finally`. Keep the existing `00:00`–`23:59` UTC window and assertions, so the test deterministically exercises the last-minute boundary while the deferred timestamp remains in the future of the real PostgreSQL clock.

Rejected option: change `computeDeferredUntil` or production quiet-hours semantics. That would broaden a test flake into user-facing behavior and is unnecessary because the failure is caused by the test's ambient `Date.now()` comparison at the half-open `23:59` boundary.
