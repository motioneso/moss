Run your money with Moss

Connect a bank and Moss builds your first budget from the last three months.

Three steps

1. Connect a bank
Sign in through Plaid. It takes about a minute per bank.
2. Moss sorts your spending
Every purchase lands in a category. You check anything from a new merchant.
3. Build your budget together
Moss proposes an amount for each category from your history. You adjust them in one chat.
What Moss can see

Read only

Moss reads balances and transactions. It never sees your bank password and cannot move real money.
