Availability and saved data

Computer sleeps when unused to free memory. Opening it wakes it. Saved files and browser data stay; apps reopen, and unsaved work may not survive a stop.

Connected viewers and active tasks keep it awake. If it cannot safely save and stop, it stays awake and tells you why.

Disabling stops Computer and keeps its saved data. Deleting data is a separate action.
