1. Your assistant

What changed. The narrow column in the middle of the screen is gone. The pane now runs the full width beside the section list, and each group sits under a ruled heading like Today's, with no boxes around it.

Why. The preview of how your assistant sounds now sits beside the persona controls on a wide screen, so you see the effect while you change it. On the phone it drops under the controls. Settings sit label-left, control-right, so the eye runs down one edge.

Renamed. The pane is "Your assistant". The "YOLO mode" heading reads "Auto-approve actions", the name of its own switch, so the screen says what it does.

2. AI providers (admin)

What changed. Each provider is one ruled line instead of a box inside a box. Name and status, how it signs in, how many models, and the actions read across in columns. One provider is shown opened to its model list.

On the phone. A provider line becomes three short lines: name and status, how it signs in, then the model count. Its buttons fold into one "more" menu, shown open on the Codex line, so nothing gets squeezed or wraps.

Renamed. The admin pane is "AI providers". "User chat override" reads "Let people choose their own chat model", and the services list is headed "Which model does each job". The classifier is called the "Quick sorter", and its "Shadow" setting is called "Trial", because those say what they do.

3. Connected accounts

What changed. Each account is one line with its health and its two switches, Email access and Calendar access, so you can see every account's state at once. An account that needs you gets a plain amber line under it saying what broke and what to do.

Adding an account. The two ways to connect stay visible at the foot of the pane as ordinary lines, not tiles, with the privacy promise beneath them.

4. Phone section picker

The problem today. On a phone, the full list of sections sits above every pane, so after you pick one you scroll past fourteen links to reach it.

The fix. Opening Settings with nothing chosen shows the full list (left). Once you pick a section, the list folds into one bar naming where you are, and the pane starts right under it (middle). Tapping the bar brings the list back as a sheet from the bottom of the screen (right).

Desktop. Nothing changes on a wide screen. The section list stays as the left column you see in the other mockups.

5. One Connections screen, instead of three

The idea. Data sources, Connected accounts and Integrations are all "things Moss can reach". Here they share one pane called Connections, with a count of each kind at the top and one section per kind below.

What you gain. One place to look when something stops syncing, and two fewer entries in the section list. What you lose. A longer pane, and the notes folder setting gets less prominence than a screen of its own.

Compare. Mockup 3 keeps three separate panes. The section lists differ: three entries under Connections there, one here.

Questions for you

  1. Numbers on the section headings? Today numbers its sections ("01 Your day, laid out") because they read in order down the day. Settings sections have no order, so these mockups keep Today's ruled heading but drop the number. Keep it that way, or add the numbers so the two screens match exactly?
  2. Three panes or one? Compare mockup 3 (Connected accounts, Data sources and Integrations stay separate) with mockup 5 (all three in one Connections pane). Which do you want?
  3. Rename "YOLO mode"? These mockups call it "Auto-approve actions", the name its switch already has. Keep that, or keep "YOLO mode" as a playful name?
  4. "Quick sorter" for the classifier? The admin screen calls it the classifier, with a "Shadow" mode that only records what it would have done. These mockups call it the "Quick sorter" with a "Trial" mode. Keep the new names, or keep the old ones because other screens and the release notes already use them?
  5. Where the provider buttons go on a wide screen. On the phone, Log in, Terminal, Edit and Remove fold into one "more" menu. On desktop they still sit in a row on each provider line. Fold them into the menu on desktop too, for a calmer list, or keep them one click away?
