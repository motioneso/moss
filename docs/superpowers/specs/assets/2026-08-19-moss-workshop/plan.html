Agreeing the plan

Before it builds anything, Moss writes down what it means to make. This is the approval that matters, because it is the one the admin has an opinion about — and it is cheap to change.

Can you build me something that shows the Good Mythical Morning and Good Mythical More videos posted today, with the video embedded?

Yes. Two things I need first.

Do you want just today's, or the last few days when they have not posted yet? And should it be on its own page, or on your Today page?

Own page, and fall back to the last three days

Right. Here is what I mean to build.

Agree this plan?
What it does
A page called Mythical in your sidebar. The newest video from each of the two channels, playing in place. If neither has posted today, the last three days instead.
What it reaches
YouTube, and nothing else.
What it keeps
Nothing. It reads the channels each time it runs.
When it runs
Every morning at 7, and whenever you open the page.
Roughly
Ten minutes to build, about 0.60 of your 5.00 daily limit.
