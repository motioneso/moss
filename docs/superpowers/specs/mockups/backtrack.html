Backtrack — screen mockups

Agreed with Ben, 2026-09-23. Backtrack is Trail Marker's text-only day memory: it reads the words in the window in front of you, keeps them in your Moss for 30 days, then folds each day into a note. Spec: 2026-09-23-trail-marker-screen-history.md (#2638).

A. Menu-bar card: Focus and Backtrack toggles

Today's card (StatusCardView). The primary button becomes Pause All / Resume All, and under it are two ordinary menu rows with a switch each, Focus and Backtrack. Each switch pauses or resumes only that feature. A row appears only once its feature is turned on in Settings, so someone who never enabled Backtrack sees just the Focus row. Pause All also stops the connection to Moss. While it's on, the switches keep their positions but are greyed and can't be changed, so Resume All brings back exactly what was running. The gold dot on the menu-bar mark shows whenever Backtrack is actually recording.

B. Trail Marker Settings → Backtrack

The existing Settings window (SettingsWindow): sidebar with an icon per section, and a grouped Form on the right, like the Focus pane. Backtrack is a new sidebar row between Focus and Permissions. The Never watch list is shared with Focus, one list in one place, so this pane links to it instead of repeating it.

C. One-time consent (when the switch is first turned on)

Shown once per Mac. "Not now" leaves the switch off. If Screen Recording isn't granted yet, "Turn on" continues to the existing permission step.

Turn on Backtrack?

Backtrack helps Moss remember your day so you can ask about it later.

  • Reads the words in the window in front of you, about every 10 seconds while it changes. Never pictures, sound or typing.
  • That includes messages people send you and pages you read.
  • Keys, card numbers and codes Trail Marker can recognise are removed on this Mac first, and password fields are never read.
  • Kept in your Moss for 30 days, then turned into a daily note.
  • Apps on your Never watch list and private windows are skipped.
  • Only you can see it. Delete any of it from Moss at any time.
