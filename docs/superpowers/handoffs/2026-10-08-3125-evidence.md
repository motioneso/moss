## Delivered behavior and approved migration

Main is a durable owner designation, separate from the most recently active conversation.
First upgrade designates the owner's most recent persistent drawer history once, ordered by
activity descending then ID ascending; older histories remain side chats. Private, module and
foreign shared histories are excluded. Main is created only when no eligible history exists.
Later side activity cannot redefine Main. The signed-in viewer's own Main is hydrated and
resumed on browser startup, including when a warm side engine exists. Explicit new, private
and resumed chats stay bound to their selected conversation. Shared History reads remain
available and never replace the viewer's Main.

App-map capability: `packages/chat/src/manifest.ts`, `chat.thread_history`.
Authoritative rulings: `../plans/2026-10-08-scheduled-proactive-migration-decisions.md`.
