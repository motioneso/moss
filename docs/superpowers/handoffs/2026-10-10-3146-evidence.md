# Issue #3146 evidence: legacy email monitoring under combined alert settings

Branch `scheduled/3146-legacy-email-monitoring`, base `integration/scheduled-proactive`. PR #3398.

## Scope

- One selection point for unsolicited email findings (`EmailFindingPolicy`), reusable by #3147.
- Each selected email finding is charged once, in a durable table, on its selection day in the
  owner's time zone. Dismissal does not refill.
- Requested responsibilities spend none of the budget and ignore a saved master-off.
- Saved caps are kept. Unsaved defaults are email 3/day, global 8/day, email hourly 1.
- Non-email sources keep their legacy rule.
