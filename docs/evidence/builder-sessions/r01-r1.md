## Findings

Material, must change:

1. **Instrumentation is assumed, never flagged as a blocker.** The accounting template (section 8) and C7 need attempt ids, execution ids and four-stage evidence (trigger, send, durable result, receipt). That record is the R01-C1 table, which is a proposal only. It does not exist on main, and unread-row absence is not receipt. Section 10 does not say this. Add an explicit prerequisite: the ledger is separately scoped future work, gated on the build go-ahead. Until it exists, those fields can only be filled by hand and receipt cannot be shown at all. Demonstrated by search: no match on main. Not a failure of any case.
2. **Delivery and cancel windows are shorter than the retry horizon.** Source gives the delivery job up to 8 retries, 15 s delay, backoff, capped at 900 s. If the backoff doubles, the total is roughly 45 minutes. R01-C2 closes delivery at due plus 5 minutes and cancel checks at due plus 10 minutes. A retry landing later would be called "unresolved" or "no delivery" and then deliver after the case closed. Either extend each window past retry exhaustion for cases that inject failure, or keep watching and re-classify. (Config read only. The exact backoff arithmetic is not verified.) A delivery more than 60 s late also carries a "late" wording in the product, so S1 and S2 must record that wording.
3. **Race interval is unsound.** "Once per variant, inside the 60 seconds before due" is not a race. The worker polls every 2 s, so only edits or cancels within a few seconds of the due moment can collide with delivery. One attempt per variant cannot show absence of a duplicate or a lost cancel. Fix: define offsets around the due time (for example minus 5 s to plus 3 s in fixed steps, covering the poll period), a fixed number of repeats, all chosen before the run and all counted. Cancel and delivery are serialised in the database, so the two legal outcomes in S4-b match the code.
4. **Cancel and silence counting is ambiguous.** C6 says a cancelled attempt is out of the numerator, but never says whether it is in the denominator. Roadmap rule: denominator is all accepted attempts, with cancelled shown separately. State it, and state the numerator and denominator with and without. Also: S4-b "delivered once and cancel reported too late" needs its own count line; an owner-cancelled attempt that correctly stays quiet must not be counted as S7 correct silence; and an attempt still open at the window end (C2) must not apply to a cancelled one.
5. **Fix-proof pair cannot be shown by the template.** A run record has one build commit in its header, C10 forbids carrying a result across a commit, and F2 forces a new window per change. Failing-before and passing-after are therefore in two different records. T4 only works inside one. Add a per-attempt "supplements attempt id" field, a cross-record link (earlier record hash plus attempt id), and a rule that the rerun uses the same case revision, inputs and race offsets, and a different build commit. Keep the original row unchanged.
6. **Open-choice inconsistencies.** (b) B2 excludes all of S9, but S9-c (cross-domain) depends on (c) only, so under B2 plus A3 the document contradicts itself. B2 also leaves no cross-owner test at all while keeping a hard stop that nothing exercises. State that explicitly or keep a minimal single-account negative check. (a) B1 lists the not-run phone rows but omits S5-d. S7 is "applicable" with no branch for (c): a one-time reminder always has something to say, so its declared "nothing new" case only exists for scheduled checks or recurring reminders. Say which, and make S7 conditional on (c) if it is the former. (d) A4 uses the word "mismatch", which is not one of pass, fail or not run (C8). Define its status and where it is counted. Counting rules otherwise do not change between branches, as required.
7. **The source trace they rely on was not re-reviewed.** T04-R1 round 2 reviewed t04-c1 r2. R01-C2 cites r3, which has no review record. Spot-checks I did (retry cap of two, SCHEDULED_SOURCE_CHANGED, tasks.get only) agree with the text. Treat r3 as accepted-pending, not independently reviewed.
8. **Distinctions missing.** #3335 (replay of an answered request, open) is not in the limitations table (T5) or on S12-g, where it belongs. #3514 (closed, real-provider sandbox proof) is not mentioned. #3078 (clean-launch only) is stated correctly in section 7. #3339, #3340, #3085 are listed as open limits, correctly. #3135, #3338 and #3419 are named as accepted exceptions, correctly.
