Moss weekly Issue #41

Week of October 5, 2026

A big week. Meetings arrived and learned to record from your Mac, chat moved over to sit beside whatever you're looking at, and Moss can now handle a lot more of the app on your behalf. Settings, Activity and Tasks all got a fresh coat too, and the fixes are at the bottom if that's all you came for.

This week's headline

Meetings, recorded from your Mac

Moss now has a Meetings page that keeps the live transcript and your own notes side by side, with a summary written when you stop. Link your Mac once, then start, pause or stop recording from a small pill on your screen, choosing whether to capture your microphone, the call audio or both. You can hide the pill and the meeting keeps going. Your notes stay private, past meetings show up in History, and meeting data is included when you export your account.

The recording also got a lot sturdier over the week. It no longer stops itself a moment after you press Start, rides out microphone hiccups without a Resume, keeps going when you join a Teams or Google Meet call in your browser, cuts down on echo from your speakers, and stops writing repeated lines during silence. Summaries use your default model and tell you plainly when they can't run.

New

Moss can do much more of the app from chat

Ask in chat and Moss can now find and use many more of the app's own actions, such as reviewing memory suggestions. Anything that deletes, including deleting a note, always shows you a card to approve first, and those cards are calmer and clearer about what happened once you decide.

PR #3071·PR #3094·PR #3091

Chat beside your page

On a wide screen, chat now makes room for itself next to the page you're on, so you can see your day while you ask about it. An Expand button lets it fill the screen, and History and private chat live in a new More menu.

PR #2941·PR #2958·PR #2961

More say over your theme

The theme editor has a new look, and your own themes can now set the colour of the navigation bar and of the header at the top of every page. Paste a palette to pick from, or click part of the preview to change that colour.

PR #3004·PR #3021

Choose your decision model

The model that sorts your mail and judges your focus can now be Cloudflare's Clef or any service compatible with Jev.

PR #3059

A model activity log for admins

Admins can see every model call Moss makes, from chat replies to background jobs, with the time, kind, model and result, and without any chat content.

PR #2900·PR #2904

Classifier trial tools for admins

While the classifier runs as a trial, admins can open a report on how often it agreed with the main chat model over the last 7, 30 or 90 days. Anyone can ask Moss in chat to delete the trial records kept about their messages, and Moss confirms before it does.

PR #2964·PR #2937

Improved

Connected tools sort themselves

When you add or refresh a connection, Moss sorts each of its tools by what it does, in the background, so there's no reviewing them one by one. Tools that only look things up or make ordinary changes run without asking, while sensitive tools still check with you, and tools that send things out do too unless you allow them to send without asking. Each connection page now uses the full width, groups tools by what they do, gives each one a single switch, and shows whether its safety check is ready.

PR #3006·PR #3009·PR #3011·PR #3014·PR #3033·PR #3045·PR #2953

Settings, redesigned

Settings now matches the rest of Moss and uses the whole screen. Connected accounts, your notes folder and services live together on one Connections page, a few pages have plainer names, and on a phone you pick a section from a menu.

PR #2930·PR #2935

One activity history

The Activity page shows every kind of work Moss did for you, with what happened, how long it took and the detail of each step. Filter by time, module or model, and your choices are remembered.

PR #2976

Catch-up shows the emails worth your time

The catch-up on Today lists each email worth knowing about, who it's from and what it says, with buttons to open it, reply, turn it into a task or dismiss it.

PR #3031

A calmer, clearer Today

The header says the right time of day and explains when a briefing isn't ready yet. Empty days get one quiet line instead of three empty messages, the smallest text is a touch larger, and the faint contour lines behind the header are back.

PR #2929·PR #2931·PR #2948·PR #3017

A refreshed Tasks page

Tasks has a park-inspired layout with a visible list index, with quick capture and both task views close at hand.

PR #2972

Your assistant, by name

If you've given your assistant a name, it now shows throughout the app, from the loading screen to the menu and settings.

PR #3032

Nicer waiting and small consistencies

The loading screen appears straight away instead of a blank page, follows your light or dark setting, and shows a gently animated logo. Every page now has the same date line under its title.

PR #2944·PR #3052·PR #3008

Chat works better with the keyboard

Opening chat puts the cursor in the message box, Escape closes it and returns you to where you were, and on a phone the Tab key stays inside the chat.
