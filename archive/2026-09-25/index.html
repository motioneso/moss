Moss weekly Issue #39

Week of September 21, 2026

This was a busy week, and most of it landed on the page you open first. Today got a full redesign for mornings and evenings, the Mac gained an early focus companion called Trail Marker, and News and Sports got quicker. A lot of background AI work also got its footing back, so briefings and email sorting should feel dependable again.

This week's headline

Today, redesigned for morning and evening

The Today page now follows its approved design from top to bottom. Mornings open with a greeting, a compact weather strip and an "In this briefing" index that jumps to each section. The morning reader has a new frame, and when your plan shifts it names the block that moved and its new time. Evenings get their own header, a recap of the day and a list of loose ends.

Evening planning is rebuilt too. You reflect on the day with a few quick choices and a note, then shape tomorrow by picking a steady, lighter or full day and naming the one thing that matters most. Before anything is saved, you review each proposed change.

New

Trail Marker for Mac (early build)

Trail Marker is a small menu-bar app that links to your Moss account. You approve it from your browser, and it shows up under Active sessions, where you can sign it out.

PR #2564·PR #2568·PR #2573

Gentle focus check-ins

During a focus block you scheduled in Moss, Trail Marker can notice which app you're in and nudge you if you've drifted. When an app's title isn't enough to go on, it can look at the screen once.

PR #2578·PR #2584

Apps Trail Marker never watches

Pick apps, like your banking app, that Trail Marker will never look at, even when it watches your whole screen.

PR #2634

A sorting model for News and Sports

Choose a small, fast model to check stories against your preferences. Refreshes get much quicker, and Moss falls back to your main model if the small one doesn't answer.

PR #2623·PR #2627·PR #2662

A redesigned Sports section on Today

Last night's scores, a lead story with a photo and tonight's games now sit together in one section. Your followed teams and leagues appear as newspaper-style clippings with their logos and colors.

PR #2558·PR #2652·PR #2666·PR #2581·PR #2567

More women's leagues

Sports now covers more women's competitions, including the Women's Pro Baseball League. The standings picker also opens on the league and division you last looked at.

PR #2669

Artwork when a story has no photo

If the lead story on Today has no photo, Moss draws a contour print in the story's topic colors so the space doesn't sit empty.

PR #2657

Improved

A simpler Trail Marker menu

One Pause All button stops everything, a separate switch pauses just Focus, and the menu wears Moss's forest green. Judge Now and Check for Updates are gone, because Trail Marker checks your focus by itself.

PR #2631·PR #2635·PR #2645·PR #2632

Dark mode that keeps its color

Every theme keeps its real accent color in dark mode, dividers are easier to see, and hover highlights use a soft tint instead of a bright block.

PR #2653·PR #2655

Sports page cards match Today

The team cards on the Sports page now share the clean look of the Today cards, with the next game written along the bottom.

PR #2667

Smarter email sorting

Mail from a standard mail account is judged by its message text, not just its subject line. Marketing mail from strangers gets set aside sooner, without an extra review.

PR #2598·PR #2600

Safer hands-off mode

When Moss runs without asking, it no longer takes risky actions like sending a reply or forgetting a memory. Those still wait for your go-ahead.

PR #2541

A trail-map texture on Today

The Today header now carries a faint trail-map pattern.
