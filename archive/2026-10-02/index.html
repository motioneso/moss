Moss weekly Issue #40

Week of September 28, 2026

This was the week Today grew up. Most of the work went into the morning and evening briefings, which now read like something a thoughtful assistant wrote rather than a pile of notes. Email got quieter and smarter, the chat model picker got favorites, and the fixes list is long enough that we gave it room to breathe.

This week's headline

A real morning report

The full morning briefing is now its own report, separate from Today. Today stays focused on what you need to do, while the report walks you through the day: events in clock order with their times and places, today's forecast, and a plain note on what to prepare.

At the bottom, the report lists every source it drew on, with a plain name, the time it was checked, and what it added. If you ever wonder why Moss said something, you can see where it came from.

New

Evening wrap-up with choices

In the evening, each open item says why it is still open and lets you move it to tomorrow, pick another day, or let it go. Finished tasks get a short note, and the plan for tomorrow points out real free time.

PR #2800·PR #2802

Open time and breaks in your schedule

The Today schedule shows the gaps between blocks, from a short break to open time, and tells you when the day's commitments are done. Task blocks are tagged Flexible or Proposed, and a preparation block says which meeting it is for.

PR #2751·PR #2799

Smarter first-meeting and errand notes

The first-meeting card mentions a real preparation block when you have one and links straight to the meeting. The morning view also names your next event with a place, so you know where you're headed later.

PR #2758·PR #2801

Re-run a briefing from chat

Ask Moss in chat to re-run one of your briefings, and it tells you when the fresh one is ready.

PR #2811

Refresh stale email before your briefing

If your email hasn't synced in over a day, you can refresh it and get a new morning briefing once it catches up.

PR #2715

Favorite models in chat

The chat model picker groups models by provider, and you can star the ones you use most to keep them at the top.

PR #2812

AI helper tools that update safely

Each AI provider card shows which version of its helper tool is installed and says clearly when it's too old for the chosen model. When a newer version arrives, Moss tries it first and keeps the current one if the test fails.

PR #2818·PR #2836

Improved

Email sorting that keeps the noise out

Moss can sort email with your chosen sorting model, and keeps receipts, confirmations and account notices out of your briefings while leaving them in your mail and search. New mail also turns up sooner during long catch-ups, and failed syncs explain themselves in plain words.

PR #2808·PR #2806·PR #2819

Needs You shows what's actually at risk

Today groups overdue and at-risk tasks and visible loose ends under Needs You, and counts them all.

PR #2719·PR #2710

More control over tomorrow

Moss previews changes to tomorrow's plan before you save them, and plans more or fewer task blocks depending on how full you say your day is.

PR #2712

Tidier morning side column

The morning view tells you when a block in your plan moved since the report was written, and stops repeating counts and agenda you can already see.

PR #2797

A steadier briefing order

Timed events come first in clock order, quiet events fold into the opening, sports always gets its own heading, and sections that only repeat an event's name are gone.

PR #2849·PR #2859

Today's news reads cleaner

The same story from several outlets now shows once, and a short "Your news, in context" note sits under the smaller stories.

PR #2750·PR #2798

Live scores show the game clock

A live score on a followed team's card now shows the quarter, period or inning, and the card keeps the same height either way.

PR #2755

The sorting model is now the classifier

Assistant settings call the sorting model the classifier, and add a new classifier switch that stays off for now.
