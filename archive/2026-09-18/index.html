Moss weekly Issue #38

Week of September 14, 2026

Today received its biggest evolution yet. Rather than just showing a list of disconnected cards, Today now guides your day from evening planning to morning briefing. A rich green hero banner welcomes you with Moss's daily assessment, weather, and schedule timeline, backed by an evening reflection flow and one-click morning briefing review. Desktop users also get a collapsible navigation rail for more focus.

This week's headline

A prepared day, from evening to morning

Today is now a complete day-planning companion. Each evening, a guided four-step flow helps you reflect on the day, gauge your energy, choose tomorrow's focus, and draft a schedule. Overnight changes are tracked automatically.

In the morning, a dedicated briefing report presents Moss's assessment, summarizes your commitments, and lets you review proposed changes with a single 'Accept all' or adjust them block by block. The main Today page features a bold dark green hero band, an editorial timeline with distinct committed and proposed blocks, a quick-action rail, and dedicated desks for News and Sports.
