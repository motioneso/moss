Moss weekly Issue #43

Week of October 26, 2026

This was a big week. Moss can now speak up on its own when something in your day needs attention, it has a proper Android app, and a lot of chat answers now come with a card you can read at a glance. The ring got much faster too, and the fixes are at the bottom if that's all you're here for.

This week's headline

Moss checks in on your day

Moss can now post a short check-in in your Main chat when something in your tasks, calendar, memory, sports or news needs your attention. When nothing has changed, it stays quiet. You choose how often it looks and which of those sources it may read, all from Settings.

Each check-in has a thumbs up and a thumbs down, and you can tell Moss to stop bringing up a topic (and change your mind later). Times in check-ins show in your own timezone, written the way a person would say them.

New

Moss for Android

Moss now has an Android app that opens Moss full screen. Ring settings live under Settings, Connections, Hardware, where you'll find delivery, ring status, storage and connection help in one place. You can connect a ring you already own, look through its saved recordings, and stop any single one from being sent.

PR #3764·PR #3586·PR #3566·PR #3592

Answers you can see

Ask about the weather and Moss draws an hourly forecast with a temperature curve, icons, rain and wind. Ask about your team and it shows the latest result with logos and the score. Questions about tasks and reminders get a tidy card with due dates, times and status, and facts you share in chat can become a labelled visual summary. Every card says where its information came from.

PR #3648·PR #3739·PR #3633·PR #3661·PR #3700·PR #3603

Every kind of recording

Meetings now takes more than meetings. Paste in a transcript and Moss will name it, pick a type, and label it personal or work. You can then change or filter that type yourself. You can also ask Moss in chat to add, rename or retire recording types and choose how each one gets summarised. When Moss isn't sure whether a recording is personal or work, it says so in Main with a link to review it.

PR #3785·PR #3790·PR #3815

Transcripts that know your words

Save spellings for names and terms, and Moss will use them to guide future meeting transcripts as well as your audio service allows. You can also set the spoken language for new transcripts or let it be detected automatically. Admins can choose one transcription provider and model for everyone.

PR #3653·PR #3594·PR #3602

Improved

A much faster ring

Holding the ring button to talk now gets a reply in about 15 seconds instead of around 50. Voice requests are picked up sooner and queued ones carry on the moment Moss is free. Ring messages also flow straight into Main chat without touching your unsent drafts, and quick voice commands no longer show up as meetings.

PR #3704·PR #3707·PR #3709·PR #3656·PR #3717

A simpler ring app

The ring app's Home now shows only the thing you need to do next, with everything else gathered in Settings. Ring setup finishes back on your Hardware settings. The reminder for a recording that has run past an hour now pops up with sound and vibration, and repeats every 30 minutes until you dismiss it.

PR #3760·PR #3635·PR #3756

Tidier AI provider settings

Adding an AI provider happens in one pop-up, and the settings you rarely touch fold away. OpenCode no longer has its own special card, so it works like every other provider. You can pick the decision model in a simple dialog and lock your own assistant to one model. The Classifier's chat gate can now be switched straight to On.

PR #3683·PR #3701·PR #3676·PR #3673

Recordings clean up after themselves

Deleting a recording or your account now clears its linked notes and memories first, and holds back any note you've changed so you can review it. Account exports now include the history behind recording notes and memories.

PR #3812·PR #3802·PR #3810

Less reading in Settings

Outlook calendar sits in a compact account row, Connections and Notes repeat themselves less, and Meeting and transcription settings keep their help short.

PR #3773·PR #3613

Turning off a module asks first

Before an add-on module is switched off, Moss now asks you to confirm and explains what happens to background jobs still waiting to run.
