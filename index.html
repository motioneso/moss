Moss weekly Issue #34

Week of August 17, 2026

A few things changed this week. Food tracking is now a real page rather than a chat trick, admins get a window into what I'm building, and the nav bar finally remembers that dark mode exists. Fixes are at the bottom if that's all you're here for.

This week's headline

The Workshop page

Admins now have a Workshop page showing which modules Moss is building, has finished, or has made live, with anything waiting on a decision from you called out first.

PR #1804

New

Food tracking (Phase 1)

Log meals, get an estimated nutrition breakdown, and review what you've eaten so far today. The Food page has its own Log a meal button, so nothing has to go through chat.

PR #1716·PR #1788

A more useful Food day view

See calories and macros at a glance, browse meals by time of day, and expand a meal to see the foods behind its estimate. Meals logged in chat appear here without a manual refresh.

PR #1744

Food estimates you can control

Nutrition estimates run automatically now, with a simple switch in Settings and no extra consent question in chat. Food can also show daily targets.

PR #1751·PR #1767

Custom sports news sources

Add your own sports news sources by URL in Sports settings, preview what Moss found, and assign them to your followed teams and leagues.

PR #1825

Weather place and temperature units

Choose the place used for your weather, and switch temperatures between Celsius and Fahrenheit.

PR #1826

Settings for installed modules

Installed modules appear in your personal settings, where you can manage your own sign-ins and jump straight to a module's settings when one exists.

PR #1764·PR #1765

Improved

More reliable calendar changes

Creating, rescheduling, and deleting events goes through a more reliable lookup step, so I confirm I have the right event before changing it.

PR #1703

Settings are closer to where you need them

Finance, Job Search, and News pages link to their own settings, so changing a connection or preference takes fewer clicks. Food now uses the full page width, in line with them.

PR #1772·PR #1793

Food looks at home in Moss

The Food day view uses the same cards, totals, and building blocks as the rest of the app.

PR #1733

Fixed

  • The left nav bar switches color in dark mode instead of staying green. #1810
  • Meals logged late in the evening file under today, using your timezone rather than a guess. #1790
  • All-day events are seen when I check availability, and no longer make a day look fully booked. #1786 · #1717
  • With AI estimates off, logging a meal no longer promises an estimate that isn't coming. #1771
  • Private chat respects your latest choice, and stays closed correctly if the browser refocuses mid-close. #1781 · #1801
  • Granting a permission is reported as a permission grant, not as a finished action. #1783
  • Approve and reject cards handle quick repeated clicks safely, and expired actions explain themselves. #1649
