Moss weekly Issue #44

Week of November 2, 2026

This was a big week for the kitchen. Meals grew from an idea into something you can plan, shop, and cook with, Finance picked up a long list of improvements, and I got better at telling you what is going wrong when something breaks. Fixes are at the bottom if that's all you're here for.

This week's headline

Meals: plan the week, shop for it, cook it

Meals is now a complete place to run your household's food. Moss can draft a week of meals through chat, and the Plan tab shows each recipe. You can lock the dishes you like, regenerate or swap the ones you don't, rearrange days, and re-plan mid-week when life changes.

Around the plan sits the rest of the kitchen. A shopping list with a Bought button subtracts what you already have in your pantry, you can rate meals and mark favourites as you cook, and each person's Never serve list is respected. Meals also replaces Food for new installs, while installed copies of Food keep working.

New

Finance, much closer to complete

Budgeting gets exact envelope amounts, carry-over, and savings targets. Transactions gain search, filters, split purchases, bulk categorising, and merchant rules you can preview and undo. Reports, settings activity, and the phone layout got a polish pass too.

PR #3874·PR #3964

Ask Moss why something is broken

Admins can ask Moss why a background job or a connection is failing, and Moss can read its own recent logs to find out. Moss can also explain why particular stories appear in your news feed.

PR #3981·PR #3988·PR #3983

Finance rules from chat

Ask Moss to always file a merchant under a category and it saves the rule right away, then tells you exactly what it did. Moss still asks before changing individual categories.

PR #3933·PR #3928

A quicker route for simple chat requests

Simple requests like turning on lights can be handled by a faster model, and Moss shows a short activity line when it does. Each account can switch this off, and locks, doors, and garage openers are left out unless you opt in.

PR #3959·PR #3962·PR #3965

Meetings home table

The Meetings page is now a table showing when each meeting was recorded and how long it ran, with a dash where the length is not known.

PR #3851

Improved

Finance starts the month sensibly

Early in the month Finance now opens on the right view, and shows a loading placeholder instead of a blank page. Category saving takes one step, and review rows start collapsed.

PR #3954·PR #3920

Meeting summaries survive Claude updates

Updates to Claude no longer stop Moss from writing your meeting summaries.

PR #4006

Settings header stays on one row

The Personal and Admin switch sits beside the search box and only stacks on narrow windows.

PR #3915

Modules can use more detailed app maps

Installed modules can describe all of their screens and settings, so Moss knows more about them.
