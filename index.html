Moss weekly Issue #35

Week of August 17, 2026

This was a week about watching Moss work. There is now a Workshop where you can see what Moss is building, agree to a plan before it starts, and try a half-finished module without inflicting it on anyone else. Elsewhere, sports news and weather picked up settings worth having, and a handful of small annoyances went away.

This week's headline

The Workshop, where Moss builds its own modules

Admins get a Workshop page listing the modules Moss is building, has finished, or has already made live, grouped so anything waiting on a decision from you comes first.

The interesting part is what happens around it. Before a build starts, chat shows a plan card in plain terms: what the module does, what it reaches out to, what it keeps, when it runs, and roughly what it costs, with buttons to agree or not. While it is being built, a module stays a draft that runs only for the person building it, so nothing half-finished shows up for anyone else until you press ship. Ask for a change mid-build and a cosmetic tweak just happens, while anything that reaches somewhere new comes back for approval first.

New

Custom sports news sources

Add your own sports news sources by URL in Sports settings, preview what Moss found before committing, and assign them to your followed teams and leagues. The default headlines stay unless you remove them, and removing them leaves scores, schedules, and standings alone.

PR #1825

Your weather place, in your units

Search for the place you want your weather from, pick the right one when several share a name, and change it later. Temperatures switch between Celsius and Fahrenheit.

PR #1826

Improved

A new look for this page

The weekly summary you are reading has a new design and is published properly again every Friday morning. It used to be built each week and quietly thrown away.

PR #1830

A job board that does not break on one bad row

The job search board now checks each role before showing it. A malformed one is left out rather than rendered broken, the rest of the board still loads, and a plain message tells you how many could not be shown so nothing disappears silently.

Fixed

  • Private chat stays closed correctly if you switch away from the app and back while it is closing. #1801
  • Quoted evidence behind a commitment shows exactly what was written, punctuation and all, rather than being mangled. #1821
  • Action history no longer records a module's tool call as successful when the module itself reported an error. #1654
